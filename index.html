Every Owl Counts

Support Our Owls: Fundraising & PBIS Store

Two direct, high-impact ways your family can fuel classroom magic and reward positive student behavior at Old Orchard Elementary.

Direct Giving & PowerUp Fun Run Tax-Deductible 501(c)(3)

Direct Giving & PledgeDrive (Pledgestar)

Family and community contributions through Pledgestar are the heartbeat of our PTO budget. Unlike traditional catalog sales where third-party vendors keep half the proceeds, direct giving keeps funds right here at Old Orchard to directly pay for:

  • ✓ Enrichment Assemblies & Field Trips that bring science, arts, and history to life for every grade level.
  • ✓ Classroom Supplies & Teacher Grants so our educators have the tools they need without paying out of pocket.
  • ✓ Community Events & Campus Spirit like our Fall Festival, Trunk or Treat, and Multicultural Night.
Donate on Pledgestar
Positive Behavior Rewards Wise Owl Choices!

Donate to the PBIS Store

Our Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS) Store celebrates and rewards Old Orchard Owls for demonstrating positive behavior, safety, respect, and responsibility across campus.

  • 1 Students earn incentives all month long for kindness, leadership, and safe choices on the playground and in class.
  • 2 Parents & supporters can purchase pre-selected reward items directly from our official Amazon Wishlist.
  • 3 Items ship straight to Old Orchard Elementary—keeping the shelves stocked and our Owls beaming with pride!

Our Commitment to Old Orchard
Soaring Higher Together

We are dedicated to promoting the education, enrichment, and well-being of every student at Old Orchard Elementary—at home, in school, and in the community—by fostering open communication and strong partnerships between parents, educators, administrators, and the community. We strive to enhance educational opportunities and facilities through volunteer programs, resource acquisition, and events not covered by standard school budgets, while raising funds to support these vital objectives.

Fostering Communication

Providing a transparent forum connecting families, teachers, and administrators to champion student well-being.

Campus Enhancement

Upgrading educational facilities and acquiring classroom learning tools that go beyond standard school budgets.

Events & Volunteers

Organizing school-wide projects, festivals, and raising funds so all Old Orchard Owls have opportunities to thrive.
